DataMind Audio is an audio software company that brings advances in sound design technology into the modern music production workflow. It was founded by a small team of electronic music producers, machine learning researchers, and audio programmers, and it has been supported by institutions including the University of Edinburgh. Its Combobulator plugin is based on the RAVE technology developed at IRCAM. The company provides sound designers and music producers with tools that expand what's possible in sound.
