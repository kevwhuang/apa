Asher Nu is the alias of Kyle Henderson, a music producer, audio engineer, and sound designer. His tracks bring together elements of electronic, orchestral, and hip-hop music. A graduate of Austin Community College's Audio Engineering program, he aims to work as a sound designer and composer for video games. He teaches one-on-one lessons, and his free Vital presets have been downloaded more than 10,000 times.
