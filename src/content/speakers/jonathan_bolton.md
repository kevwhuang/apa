Jonathan Bolton is an executive producer from Austin, Texas, with a strong operational background and roots as a musician. He focuses on the business of live entertainment and on leading teams. He holds a master's from Berklee College of Music and a bachelor's from the McCombs School of Business at The University of Texas at Austin. He has more than a decade of experience across multiple industries, including music, tech, and education. Jonathan enjoys building community and paying it forward to the next generation.
