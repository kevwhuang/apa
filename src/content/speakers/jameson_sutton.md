name: Jameson Sutton

Jameson Sutton has spent more than a decade shaping how games sound, with credits including Sonic Mania, Job Simulator, and Penny's Big Breakaway. His work spans sound design, implementation, audio systems, and creative direction, which gives him a view of both the artistic and technical sides of game audio. He is especially interested in how good tools, smart workflows, and expressive sound design come together to make memorable interactive experiences.
