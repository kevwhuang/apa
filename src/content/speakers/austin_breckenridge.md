---
name: Austin Breckenridge
---

Austin Breckenridge, co-founder and executive director of The A&R Foundation, manages Austin-based artists Britny Lobas and Adam Knight. Originally from San Jose, California, he began his music career at 17 by organizing live events across the San Francisco Bay Area. In 2010 he founded Nemesis Media Inc., an independent record label for emerging Bay Area artists, which released 10 albums, produced two local festivals, and expanded to Austin, Texas, in 2012. He served as CEO until the company's successful exit in 2015 and has since held finance roles across Texas in small-business and commercial banking, community development, and business advising. Across a career of more than 17 years, he has worked as an artist manager, tour manager, business manager, and event producer.
