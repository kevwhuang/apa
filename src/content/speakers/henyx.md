Henyx, the alias of Braulio "Lio" Guerra, comes from Houston and shares his expertise with the Austin community. Drawing on nearly a decade of experience, he works directly with Image-Line, the creators of FL Studio, to inspire the next generation of producers and make music production accessible to all. His credits include the video game Geometry Dash. A father and an avid synthesist, he encourages other producers to push beyond their limits.
