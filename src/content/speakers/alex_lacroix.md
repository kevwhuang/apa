name: Alex LaCroix

Alex LaCroix is an Austin-based songwriter, producer, drummer, and creative director. As a co-writer and co-producer, he has contributed to a growing catalog of indie pop, rock, and singer-songwriter releases that have together earned more than 500,000 streams on Spotify. His work connects songwriting and production, with a focus on collaboration, artist development, and taking ideas from concept to finished record.
