name: Lonely Child
---

Lonely Child is the alias of Bryan Ray, a producer, songwriter, mixer, and musician based in Austin, Texas. His original songwriting, productions, and mixes have drawn tens of millions of streams and earned multiple placements across TV and film, including Fox and Netflix. He co-writes and co-produces with Daddy NAT and has produced most of Daddy NAT's catalog, and he has co-written and co-produced for Jefferson Clay. He also produces for MIDDLESPOON and is a member of the band. He was nominated twice for Producer of the Year at the Austin Music Awards, in 2017 and 2018.
