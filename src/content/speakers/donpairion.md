name: DONPAIRION
---

A progressive tech house producer, DONPAIRION has spent more than six years making grooves and mentoring emerging artists. He found house and techno in the EDM scenes of Austin and Los Angeles, where the dance floor led him into DJing, production, and creative direction. The DONPAIRION name dates from the early 2020s, and his 2021 move to Los Angeles sped up his study of the EDM ecosystem, from mix engineering to artist branding. Back in Texas, he leads PH7 Records and the Finding Balance in EDM podcast, and he runs workshops and showcases that put music, community, and creative sustainability first.
