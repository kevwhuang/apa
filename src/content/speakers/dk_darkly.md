Collin McLaughlin, who records as dk darkly, is an Austin-based electronic music producer best known for his "hybrid mashup" remix projects. One of them, TOGETHER, was featured on Bandcamp's top 50 best-selling electronic releases for three consecutive weeks in 2026. He has been producing music for more than 20 years, blending genres such as French house, hyperpop, funk, and prog rock.
