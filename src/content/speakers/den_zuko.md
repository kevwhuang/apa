---
name: Den Zuko
---

Brendan Carroll has been producing an eclectic mix of music for more than 14 years, and as Den Zuko he has releases on Talldoor Records and Blueprints Records. He studied jazz saxophone in college and performed with bands as a freelancer before turning to electronic music. For eight years before moving to Austin, he taught music to students of all ages. He studies music production and audio engineering at Austin Community College and performs as half of Rainbow Drip with his friend Eric Howard, known as ThinkR.
