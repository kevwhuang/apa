name: Will Evans
---

Will Evans, a musician and producer from Austin, Texas, joined his first band in 2002 while studying photography at St. Edward's University. He has since played with Bruce Castleberry (Vallejo), Xavier Muriel (Buckcherry), Zak Loy (Live), Julian Mandrake (Blue October), and Mark Younger-Smith (Billy Idol), among others, and toured with the underground industrial punk band Street Sects. His projects include the psychedelic rock duo The Swift Drag; The Sea Suns, a long-distance indie pop outfit in the style of The Postal Service; and his work as Lex Echo, a multi-genre bedroom producer and artist. In 2019 he moved into music licensing with a six-month online course covering all aspects of sync licensing. He now focuses on self-producing music for film, TV, and ads, with sync credits including Netflix, The CW, and NASCAR.
