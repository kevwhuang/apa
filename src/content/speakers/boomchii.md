Adrian Vasquez, known as Boomchii, is an audio and mixing engineer whose professional experience spans more than 15 years. He has worked with artists across Latin America and the United States, and his background includes vocal coaching, session guitar for major U.S. record labels, and live mixing at venues throughout South Texas. In 2024 he co-founded ArtistDev with music producer David Chavera and artist manager Ricco Rodriguez. The platform helps South Texas artists advance their careers and earn a sustainable income from their talent.
