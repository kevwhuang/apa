name: Kai Castro
---

Kai Castro is a Chicago-based DJ and producer. Before moving to Chicago in 2024, he played regularly at Austin venues such as The Concourse Project and Coconut Club. He has released music on several melodic and progressive labels, and his tracks have drawn support from well-known artists. Traditionally a melodic, deep, and progressive house artist, Kai has been expanding into faster party genres, including UK garage and Eurodance.
