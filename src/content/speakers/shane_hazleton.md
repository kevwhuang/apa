name: Shane Hazleton
---

Shane Hazleton is an educator and producer who has worked in music technology and production with companies such as Alesis, Numark, and Akai. He originated the concept behind the APC40, the predecessor of all modern clip-launch matrix-based controllers, including Ableton's Push, and he collaborated with LMFAO before "Party Rock Anthem" became a number-one hit. He graduated at the top of his class in Music, Interdisciplinary Computing, and the Arts from UC San Diego, where he studied under Miller Puckette, the creator of Max/MSP. He also holds a master's degree in Experimental Sound Practices and Integrated Media from CalArts. Shane teaches his own classes and works one-on-one with clients on mixing their own songs and developing their production skills.
