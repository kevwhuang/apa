DJing and producing go hand in hand but are very different skills, each of which takes time to develop. For producers taking their first steps as DJs, this workshop covers the gear and how to approach a gig, transition between tracks, and produce DJ-friendly music.

Live demonstrations on industry-standard Pioneer DJ equipment run alongside the discussion, and attendees are encouraged to test their skills by trying live transitions. Anyone who wants to mix their own music is asked to bring it on a flash drive, ideally formatted for rekordbox.
