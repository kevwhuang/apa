DREAMSHIFT taught three techniques for making melodic color bass with vocoders, harmonic sweeteners, and convolution reverb. The workshop started by synthesizing a basic dubstep sound, then used those effects to shape its heaviness into melodic tones that blend into a track, and ended by turning the result into a preset template. Notes on mixing and sound design came up along the way.
