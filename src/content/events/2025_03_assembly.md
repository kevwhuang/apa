Producers learn three techniques for making melodic color bass with vocoders, harmonic sweeteners, and convolution reverb. The process starts with synthesizing a basic dubstep sound, and those effects then shape its heaviness into melodic tones that blend into a track. DREAMSHIFT shares notes on mixing and sound design throughout and turns the finished sound into a preset template.
