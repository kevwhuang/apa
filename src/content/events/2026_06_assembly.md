Alex LaCroix, a songwriter and producer, led an interactive workshop on turning a melody into a finished song. He and the room worked in real time to develop the lyrics, shape the story, and bring the song to life.

Producers, songwriters, and artists contributed ideas and worked alongside one another, and together they helped create an original song. Along the way the session showed how a song comes together, from the first idea to the finished piece.
