description: |-
  Austin Breckenridge gave an introduction to turning creative ideas into structured business plans. The workshop was for independent musicians, visual artists, and other creative professionals who wanted to understand how business planning could support their work. He covered the basics of defining a value proposition, identifying an audience, and weighing different ways to earn income, and touched on how understanding the market and setting clear goals could guide creative decisions.

  The workshop left time for discussion and reflection, so attendees could think about how these ideas applied to their own creative projects. It gave them a starting point for business planning that supported both their creativity and their professional growth.
speakers:
  - name: Austin Breckenridge
    bio: Austin Breckenridge is the co-founder and Executive Director of The A&R Foundation and the artist manager for Austin-based Britny Lobas and Adam Knight. Originally from San Jose, California, he began his music career at 17 by organizing live events across the San Francisco Bay Area. In 2010 he founded Nemesis Media Inc., an independent record label for emerging Bay Area artists, which released ten albums, produced two local festivals, and expanded to Austin, Texas in 2012. He served as CEO until the company's successful exit in 2015. Over a 17-year career he has worked as an artist manager, tour manager, business manager, and event producer, and since 2015 he has worked in finance, in small business banking, commercial banking, community development, and business advising across Texas.
