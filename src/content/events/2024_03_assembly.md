Den Zuko demonstrated how he wrote memorable, functional, and interesting electronic music from scratch, with tips on workflow, melodies, form, and harmonies. He showed how templates, reference tracks, theme variations, and creative sampling could spark ideas and turn the inkling of an idea into a full track. He also explained how strong themes and a direction could help producers work through creative blocks and produce something worth listening to.
