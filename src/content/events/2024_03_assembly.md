Writing memorable, functional, and interesting electronic music from scratch is the focus of this session, which offers tips on workflow, melodies, form, and harmonies. Den Zuko demonstrates his process, showing how templates, reference tracks, theme variations, and creative sampling can spark an idea and turn it into a finished track. He also explains how strong themes and a clear direction can help producers work through creative blocks and make music worth listening to.
