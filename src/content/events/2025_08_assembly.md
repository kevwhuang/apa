venue: Capital Factory

Independent musicians, visual artists, and other creative professionals learn how to turn ideas into structured business plans in this workshop. It covers the basics of defining a value proposition, identifying an audience, and weighing different ways to earn income. It also touches on how understanding the market and setting clear goals can guide creative decisions.

Attendees discuss and reflect on how these ideas apply to their own projects. They gain a starting point for business planning that supports both their creativity and their professional growth.
