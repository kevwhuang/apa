A sample's basic harmonies can feel limiting. With jazz theory, a producer can reharmonize the sample by changing the bass notes under it, opening up new possibilities. Producers learn the key techniques, how to tell when reharmonization is possible, and how to keep a wide harmonic vocabulary on hand for the next sample flip.
