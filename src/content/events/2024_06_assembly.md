This class is a broad discussion of the avenues in music licensing and the steps artists take to prepare their music for it. Topics include what makes a song licensable, who the sync agencies and other players are, and how music is delivered. Artists also learn about file types and metadata, ownership splits and contracts, and performing rights organization (PRO) registration.
