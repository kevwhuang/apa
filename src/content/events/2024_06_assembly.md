Will Evans led a class built around a broad discussion of the avenues available in music licensing and the steps and procedures artists had to prepare in order to license their music. He covered what made a song licensable, who the sync agencies and other players involved were, and which music delivery methods were available. The class also went through file types and metadata, ownership splits and contracts, performance rights organization (PRO) registration, and much more.
