excerpt: This session applied jazz theory to samples with basic harmonies, changing the bass notes underneath and showing when a sample could be reharmonized.
description: Material Soul showed how jazz theory can open up a sample whose basic harmonies leave a producer little to work with. By changing the bass notes under the sample, he reharmonized it and brought out harmonic options that were not there before. He taught the key techniques, how to tell when a sample can be reharmonized, and how to keep a wide harmonic vocabulary on hand for the next sample flip.
