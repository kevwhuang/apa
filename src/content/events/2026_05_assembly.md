VonRyk walked through his mastering chain and process. He covered the fundamentals of finalizing a track after the mix and preparing it for release. He also showed how to make music sit alongside the commercial tracks played in DJ sets, just as loud and just as polished. Mastering is the last step between a finished mix and a release.
