  The workshop addressed what it was like to jump into something new without clear direction, which could be fun at first but could quickly spiral out of control and leave a producer feeling lost, frustrated, and demotivated. Henyx taught how to use FL Studio from the ground up and what it took to bring ideas to life, with hands-on experience with the software alongside audio professionals.

  Attendees were asked to bring their own laptops to follow along during the session. Experienced producers were paired with beginners so everyone got the support they needed.

  Gear was up for grabs for those who took part in the activities, with a chance to win a license for FL Studio Producer Edition ($180), a Novation FLkey Mini MIDI Controller ($120), and more, whether attendees were just getting started or already making hits.
