Frequency modulation (FM) synthesis is the subject of this concise, step-by-step walkthrough, in which Shane Hazleton builds a one-operator FM synthesizer from scratch. He draws the material from one of his sound design modules and demonstrates the key components with Ableton's Operator device.

To follow along, attendees can download the Max demo or use Max for Live, which comes with Ableton Live Suite and is shown in the session. Anyone without Ableton Live can use its demo.
