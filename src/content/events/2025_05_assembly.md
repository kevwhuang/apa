Jonathan Bolton introduced key 2025 trends in the live music and entertainment business. He covered the fundamentals of live music, including stakeholders, revenue streams, and production processes, as they apply in both festival and club settings. As a bonus, he and local producer Saratonin shared advice on getting booked and securing opportunities in live events.
