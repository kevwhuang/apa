This overview introduces the fundamentals of the live music and entertainment business, including stakeholders, revenue streams, and production processes, as they apply in both festival and club settings. It also covers key trends for 2025. Jonathan Bolton and local producer Saratonin share advice on getting booked and finding opportunities in live events.
