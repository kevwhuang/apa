The room builds a track together from scratch, creating a bassline, a melody, and drums, then arranging them into a full song. The workshop covers writing MIDI notes, picking synth sounds, importing samples, and adding effects, along with basic mixing in the arrangement view.

It suits anyone new to music production or stuck trying to finish a first track, as well as artists who want to understand production for smoother collaborations. Attendees are asked to bring headphones, so they can hear their own music in the room, and a laptop with the free 30-day trial of Ableton Live 12 installed.
