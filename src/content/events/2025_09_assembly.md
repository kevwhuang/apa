House producers learn to turn rigid kick and bass patterns into a groove in this workshop, which aims to help them break through writer's block. DONPAIRION covers selecting drums with intention, shaping sounds in audio files, using velocity in MIDI, and applying swing to loosen the feel.

He then explains how to layer drums with EQ and envelopes, shape and sidechain the bass, and balance mix levels across the kick, claps, hats, and bass. To finish, he walks the room through expanding a loop of drums and bass into a full song structure.
