Moving between tracks in a DJ set comes down to phrasing, beatmatching, EQ and filter moves, and loop-based blends. This lab breaks each one down with live demonstrations throughout. Producers learn to choose a transition that fits the energy of a set and to prepare their own tracks so they mix cleanly.
