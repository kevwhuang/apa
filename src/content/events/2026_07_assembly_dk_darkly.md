  Starting from the experience of hearing a song and immediately sensing what it could be, DJ and producer dk darkly broke down the remix process in this interactive workshop, exploring its technical, creative, and business dimensions.

  The session moved through identifying key, tempo, and groove, sourcing stems, selecting samples, and balancing new ideas with the source material. dk darkly also unpacked the logistics of releasing an unofficial remix in a copyright-mad world, drawing on his own Ableton projects to illustrate the process throughout.

  The workshop was aimed at attendees who had never attempted a remix as well as those who had been refining their craft for years, and was designed to give them a practical toolkit for their next project.
