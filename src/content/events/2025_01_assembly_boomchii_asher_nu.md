  In The Art of Vocal Production: Mixing Essentials, Boomchii and the ArtistDev team gave a presentation on vocal mixing techniques tailored for client projects. The session explored essential strategies and best practices to enhance vocal tracks, emphasizing clarity, balance, and impact, for producers from the experienced to those just starting out.

  In Video Game Sound Design Tips, Asher Nu offered insights into designing sound for a short video game cinematic cutscene, focusing on enhancing the scene through the addition and manipulation of existing sound effects. Key tips and techniques bridged the worlds of sound design and music production, with practical tools and insights for both sound designers and music producers.
