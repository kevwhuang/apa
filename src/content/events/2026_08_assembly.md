DONPAIRION led a practical session for music producers on building the ecosystem around their music. He covered defining a brand, identifying the labels, venues, and festivals that actually fit a producer's sound, growing the right industry relationships, and pulling all of it into a release strategy. The session was set up so that attendees left knowing what to do next. An ecosystem in this sense is everything around the music itself, from the brand to the industry relationships behind it.
