  Alt Slauson led a discussion on the album as a form. He made the case that albums were not dead and were more than collections of songs or glorified playlists. The discussion looked at what making an album as a whole can do, worked on the creative muscle needed to shape a narrative across one, and asked how each track could add to something greater than the sum of its parts.
