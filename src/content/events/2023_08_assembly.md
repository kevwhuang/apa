Lonely Child presented on making music on commission and why it is hard work: the client is probably not a musician, and it can be difficult to know where to even start. He broke down the process of delivering a great track to someone whose needs seem hard to meet. He also covered how a producer can take control of any situation in the studio. A commission is music made to order for a client rather than for the producer's own release.
