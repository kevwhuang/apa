description: |-
  Justin M led a discussion on how AI was changing the way music was made. It asked whether producers were using AI in their workflows and what for, how it would change music, and whether it added to or reduced creativity. The technology was moving quickly at the time, with companies such as Google, Meta, and OpenAI and artists such as David Guetta, Grimes, and DJ Jazzy Jeff working with it.

  He went through a range of AI tools to work out which were most relevant to processing vocals, processing drums and percussion, and composing melodies and harmonies. The session also took up the ongoing debate about creativity and asked what the role of human artists would be if AI could generate music from scratch.
