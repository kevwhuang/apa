description: |-
  The discussion looked at how AI was enhancing the way music was made, asking whether producers were using AI in their workflows, what it was used for, how it would change the music landscape, and whether it enhanced or reduced creativity. The technology was evolving rapidly, with companies such as Google, Meta, and OpenAI and artists such as David Guetta, Grimes, and DJ Jazzy Jeff innovating within it.

  The session examined various AI tools to determine which were the most relevant in processing vocals, processing drums and percussion, and composing melodies and harmonies. It also took up the ongoing debate surrounding creativity, asking what the role of human artists would be if AI could generate music from scratch.
