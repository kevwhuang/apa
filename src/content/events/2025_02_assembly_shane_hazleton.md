description: |-
  Shane Hazleton gave a concise walkthrough of one of his sound design modules, on frequency modulation (FM) synthesis. He went step by step through building a one-operator FM synthesizer from scratch and demonstrated the key components of FM synthesis with Ableton's Operator device.

  Participants were encouraged to download the demo of Max MSP or, if they had Ableton Live Suite, to use Max for Live, which he demonstrated. A demo version of Ableton was another option for anyone who needed it.
speakers:
  - name: Shane Hazleton
    bio: Shane Hazleton is an educator and producer who has worked in music technology and production with companies like Alesis, Numark, and Akai. He originated the concept behind the APC40, the predecessor of all modern clip-launch matrix-based controllers, including Ableton's Push, and he collaborated with LMFAO before "Party Rock Anthem" became a three-month chart topper. He graduated at the top of his class in Music, Interdisciplinary Computing, and the Arts from UCSD, where he studied under Miller Puckette, the creator of Max MSP, and holds a master's degree in Experimental Sound Practices and Integrated Media from CalArts. Shane teaches his own classes and works one-on-one with clients, helping them mix their own songs and develop their production skills.
