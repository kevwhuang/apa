description: |-
  Shane Hazleton gave a concise walkthrough of one of his sound design modules, focusing on frequency modulation (FM) synthesis. The session included a step-by-step guide to building a one-operator FM synthesizer from scratch, and key components of FM synthesis were demonstrated using Ableton's Operator device.

  Participants were encouraged to download the demo of Max MSP or, if they had Ableton Live Suite, use Max for Live, which Shane Hazleton demonstrated. A demo version of Ableton was another option if needed.
speakers:
  - name: Shane Hazleton
    bio: |-
      Shane Hazleton is an educator and producer with an impressive career in the music technology and production industry. He has worked with companies like Alesis, Numark, and Akai, and was the originator of the concept behind the APC40, the predecessor of all modern clip-launch matrix-based controllers, including Ableton's Push. Shane also collaborated with LMFAO before their "Party Rock Anthem" became a three-month chart topper.

      A graduate at the top of his class in Music, Interdisciplinary Computing, and the Arts from UCSD, Shane studied under Miller Puckette, the creator of Max MSP. He also holds a master's degree in Experimental Sound Practices and Integrated Media from CalArts.

      Shane teaches his own classes and works directly with clients one-on-one, helping them mix their own songs and develop their production skills.
