  Alt Slauson led a lab on moving between tracks in a DJ set. He broke down phrasing, beatmatching, EQ and filter moves, and loop-based blends, with live demonstrations throughout. The workshop showed how to choose a transition that fits the energy of a set and how producers can prepare their own tracks so they mix cleanly. A transition in a DJ set is the stretch where one track hands off to the next.
