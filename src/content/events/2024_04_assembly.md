title: Harnessing the Power of AI in Music Production
venue: Capital Factory

AI is changing the way music is made, and the technology moves quickly. Companies including Google, Meta, and OpenAI are working with it, as are artists such as David Guetta and Grimes. This discussion asks whether producers use AI in their workflows, what they use it for, and where it is taking music.

The room examines a range of AI tools to identify the most relevant ones for processing vocals, drums, and percussion, and for composing melodies and harmonies. Producers also take up the ongoing debate over whether the technology adds to or takes away from creativity and consider what role human artists would have if AI could generate music from scratch.
