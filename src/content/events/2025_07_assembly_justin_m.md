description: |-
  Justin M started from his own first time opening Ableton, when he was overwhelmed and had no idea what he was looking at, and from the live walkthroughs that broke things down and gave him the confidence to start creating. This workshop worked the same way.

  The group built a track together from scratch, creating a bassline, melody, and drums and arranging everything into a full song. It covered writing MIDI notes, picking synth sounds, importing samples, adding effects, and basic mixing in the arrangement view.

  Attendees were asked to bring a laptop with the free 30-day trial of Ableton Live 12 installed and headphones, so they could hear their own music in the room. It was for anyone new to music production or stuck trying to finish a first track, and for artists who wanted to understand production for smoother collaborations.
