description: |-
  Justin M drew on his own experience of opening Ableton for the first time, when he was overwhelmed and had no idea what he was looking at, and on the live walkthroughs that broke things down and gave him the confidence to start creating, which was what this workshop was all about.

  The group built a track together from scratch, learning how to create a bassline, melody, and drums and how to arrange everything into a full song. The session covered writing MIDI notes, picking synth sounds, importing samples, adding effects, and basic mixing using the arrangement view.

  Attendees were asked to bring a laptop with the free 30-day trial of Ableton Live 12 installed and a pair of headphones so they could hear their own music while working in the room.

  The workshop was for anyone new to music production, stuck trying to finish a first track, or artists wanting to better understand how production worked for smoother collaborations.
