description: Will Evans led a class built around a broad discussion of the avenues available in music licensing and the steps and procedures artists had to prepare in order to license their music. He covered what made a song licensable, who the sync agencies and other players involved were, and which music delivery methods were available. The class also went through file types and metadata, ownership splits and contracts, performance rights organization (PRO) registration, and much more.
speakers:
  - name: Will Evans
    bio: Will Evans is a musician and producer from Austin, Texas. His projects include the psychedelic rock duo The Swift Drag, the long-distance, Postal Service style indie pop outfit The Sea Suns, and his work as a multi-genre bedroom producer and artist, Lex Echo. He joined his first band in 2002 while studying photography at St. Edward's University and has since played with musicians such as Bruce Castleberry (Vallejo), Xavier Muriel (Buckcherry), Zak Loy (Live), Julian Mandrake (Blue October), and Mark Youngersmith (Billy Idol), and toured with the underground industrial punk band Street Sects. In 2019 he moved into music licensing with a six-month online course covering all aspects of sync licensing, and he now focuses on self-producing music for film, TV, and ads.
