speakers:
  - name: Will Evans
    bio: |-
      Will Evans is a musician/producer from Austin, TX. At any given time Will can be found juggling multiple artist projects that include a psychedelic rock duo (The Swift Drag), a long-distance Postal Service style indie pop outfit (The Sea Suns), and working as a multi-genre bedroom producer/artist (Lex Echo). While attending college at St. Edward's University and pursuing a degree in photography, Will joined his first band in 2002 and has since played with notable musicians such as Bruce Castleberry (Vallejo), Xavier Muriel (Buckcherry), Zak Loy (Live), Julian Mandrake (Blue October), Mark Youngersmith (Billy Idol), and toured with underground industrial punk band Street Sects.

      In 2019, Will got the itch to branch out into other avenues of the music business, which led him into the world of music licensing. After spending six months in an immersive online course dedicated to learning all aspects of sync licensing, Will continues to hone his music production skills, with a focus on self-producing quality music for use in film, TV, and ads.
