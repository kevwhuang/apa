description: |-
  Kai Castro covered the first steps of getting started as a DJ. He treated DJing and producing as skills that went hand in hand but were vastly different, each one taking time to improve at. He went through the gear involved, how to approach a gig, how to transition between tracks, and how to produce DJ-friendly music.

  The session mixed discussion with live instructor demos on industry-standard Pioneer DJ equipment, and attendees were encouraged to test their skills by trying live transitions during the workshop. Those who wanted to mix their own music were asked to bring their audio on a flash drive, with bonus points if it was formatted for rekordbox.
