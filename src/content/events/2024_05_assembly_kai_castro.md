description: |-
  Treating DJing and producing as skills that went hand in hand but were vastly different, each requiring time to improve at, Kai Castro covered the first steps in getting started on a DJ journey, including what gear was involved, how to approach a gig, how to transition between tracks, and how to produce DJ-friendly music.

  The session included discussions as well as live instructor demos on industry-standard Pioneer DJ equipment. Attendees were encouraged to test their skills by participating in live transitions during the workshop, and those who wanted to mix their own music were asked to bring their audio on a flash drive, with bonus points if it was formatted for rekordbox.
