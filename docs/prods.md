# Prods — Virtual Currency Proposal

Austin Producer Alliance · Third Draft · June 13, 2026

## Overview

We're introducing Prods, a virtual currency that rewards members for showing up, contributing, and helping our community grow. Each Prod is worth $1.00 and can be redeemed as credit toward any item in our inventory, so every contribution translates directly into something members can hold in their hands.

## Onboarding

To make that first contribution effortless, we're rolling out a new check-in system for every meet. Guests scan the welcome QR code, accept the terms and conditions, and check in with their email. That email becomes their identity on our platform, where an account is created automatically with 1 welcome Prod already in it. From there, members are signed up for our seasonal newsletter and receive a welcome email with a short form to complete their profile.

## Technical

Behind the scenes, we track everything through our platform and backend, keeping most of the process automated with light manual oversight where it's needed. Prods are tied directly to each member's account, keeping redemption simple and reliable.
