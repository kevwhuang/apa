# Austin Producer Alliance

Community site for the Austin Producer Alliance, an organization of music producers in Austin, Texas. Features a waveform timeline hero, Assembly, Hangout, and Bash pages listing events from Markdown event, speaker, and sponsor collections, a member directory and home page roster served from Supabase profiles with Spotlight track players, a photo gallery with a fullscreen viewer, live track feedback and submission boards with uploads, a Web Audio plugin rack, a store with a variation-aware cart, promo code, Prods redemption, and Stripe checkout, a resources library with timed unlocks, Prods rewards with rotating-code door check-in, a partners page, a read-only content API, a guided site tour, an admin console, a light and dark theme, floating player, realtime member chat, and AI assistant docks, a newsletter with one-click unsubscribe, contact and welcome email through Resend, custom 404 and 500 pages, and Supabase accounts at /signin with portrait uploads across onboarding and settings, signed-in redirects, and row-level security as the data boundary. Built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Three.js, Supabase, Stripe, Resend, and Anthropic. Deployed on Netlify, with the assistant's site index refreshed after each production deploy.
