# Austin Producer Alliance

Community site for the Austin Producer Alliance, an organization of music producers in Austin, Texas. Features a waveform timeline hero, Assembly, Hangout, and Bash pages listing events from Markdown event, program, speaker, and sponsor collections, a member directory with synthesized track players, a photo gallery with a fullscreen viewer, track feedback and submission dropzones, a Web Audio plugin rack, a mock storefront with a variation-aware cart, promo code, and checkout, a resources library with timed unlocks, Prods rewards with door check-in, a partners page, a read-only content API, a guided site tour, an admin console, a light and dark theme, floating player, member chat, and AI assistant docks, custom 404 and 500 pages, and client-side mock accounts at /signin with portrait uploads across onboarding and settings, signed-in redirects, and localStorage-only persistence. Built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, and Three.js. Deployed on Netlify.
